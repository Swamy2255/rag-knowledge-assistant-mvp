# Expense Reimbursement

## What you can claim

You can claim travel, meals during business travel, approved software, and office supplies bought for work. The daily meal limit while travelling is $65. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $180 per night in major cities and $140 elsewhere. Personal items, alcohol, and traffic fines are not reimbursable.

## How to submit

Submit expenses in the Finance portal at https://finance.company.example within 30 days of the expense date. Attach an itemised receipt for every claim over $25. Claims without a receipt are rejected automatically. Enter the cost centre code from your manager; the default cost centre is shown on your team page.

## Approval and payment

Your manager approves claims within 5 business days. Approved claims are paid in the next weekly payment run, on Fridays, directly to your bank account. If a claim is rejected, you will receive an email with the reason, and you can correct and resubmit it within 14 days.

## Corporate card

If you hold a corporate card, charges appear in the Finance portal automatically. You must still attach receipts and assign a cost centre within 14 days of the charge date. Unreconciled charges older than 30 days are reported to your manager.
