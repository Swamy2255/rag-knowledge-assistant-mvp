# Leave and Time Off Policy

## How much leave you get

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus 10 paid public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 1.67 days per month. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount. Unused annual leave up to 5 days may be carried into the next year; anything above 5 days expires on December 31.

## Requesting leave

Submit leave requests in the HR portal at https://hr.company.example at least 5 business days before the start date for absences of 3 days or more, and at least 2 business days before for shorter absences. Your manager must approve the request in the portal. Requests are approved in the order they are received, and no more than 2 people from the same team may be on leave on the same day without manager exception.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. You do not need a doctor's note for absences of 2 days or fewer. For absences of 3 days or more, upload a medical certificate to the HR portal within 5 days of returning. Notify your manager by 9:00 AM on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave, and secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Contact HR Operations at least 30 days before the planned start date to arrange coverage.
