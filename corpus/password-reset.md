# Resetting Your Password

## Self-service reset

If you forgot your password, use the self-service password reset page at https://password.company.example. You will need your username and access to your registered recovery email or phone. A reset link is valid for 30 minutes. After three failed attempts, the account locks for 15 minutes.

## What to do if self-service fails

If you do not have access to your recovery email or phone, contact the IT Help Desk at extension 4357 or email helpdesk@company.example. The Help Desk will verify your identity with your employee ID and a manager confirmation, then issue a temporary password. Temporary passwords expire after 24 hours and must be changed at first login.

## Password rules

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and include an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a special character. Passwords expire every 90 days. You cannot reuse any of your previous 5 passwords.

## Multi-factor authentication

After resetting your password, you will be prompted to re-register multi-factor authentication (MFA) if your MFA device was also reset. Use the Authenticator app to scan the QR code shown on screen. If you lost your MFA device, the Help Desk can reset MFA after identity verification.
