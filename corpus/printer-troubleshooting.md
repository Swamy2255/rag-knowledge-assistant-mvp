# Printer Troubleshooting

## Printer not responding

First, check that the printer is powered on and shows "Ready" on its display. If the display shows an error such as "Paper jam" or "Low toner", follow the on-screen instructions. If the printer is on but jobs do not print, restart the print spooler: on Windows, open Services, find "Print Spooler", and click Restart. Then try printing again.

## Adding a network printer

On Windows, go to Settings, then Bluetooth & devices, then Printers & scanners, and click "Add device". Select the printer by its location name, for example "Floor-3-East". On Mac, go to System Settings, then Printers & Scanners, click the + button, and select the printer from the list. You must be on the office network or VPN to see the printers.

## Print quality problems

Faded or streaked output usually means low toner or a dirty print head. For laser printers, remove the toner cartridge, gently rock it side to side, and reinsert it. For inkjet printers, run the "Clean print head" utility from the printer software. If the problem continues after cleaning, submit a ticket to Workplace Services with the printer name and a photo of the output.

## Scanning to email

Place the document face down on the scanner glass or face up in the feeder. On the printer touchscreen, choose "Scan to Email", enter your company email address, and press Start. Scanned files arrive as PDF attachments within a few minutes. If the scan does not arrive, check your spam folder before raising a ticket.
